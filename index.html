She Whom I Love

A poem by Jason

Because you came into my life, my heart found a reason to smile,

Right beside you, even ordinary moments feel worthwhile,

Every thought of you makes my heart beat a little faster,

Nothing compares to the warmth you bring into my world,

Darling, you’re a beautiful chapter I never want to end,

And if love is a journey, I’d choose you again and again.

With love — Jason
